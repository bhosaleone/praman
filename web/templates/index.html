Praman Algebraic Contract (MATH.md & NO_API.md)

1. The Pinned Voice Formula (Eq. 6)

demand(s) = 0.50·breadth(s) + 0.25·coverage(s) + 0.15·density(s) + 0.10·depth(s)

The denominator does NOT move. If a voice is unmeasured (⊥), its weight is not redistributed to surviving voices. The measured mass \(c(s) < 1.0\) caps the achievable score below 1.0 (Theorem 2) and flags the score as partial.

2. Evidence Banding

Scores are transparent indicators of autocomplete evidence within this run:

  • Strong Evidence: Demand Index ≥ 0.50. Broad expansion yield, prominent rank position, or question demand.
  • Moderate Evidence: Demand Index ≥ 0.25. Measurable presence on partial expansion axes.
  • Weak Evidence: Demand Index < 0.25. Sparse expansion surface.
  • Insufficient / Unmeasured: Zero voices could be fetched (network error/429/timeout). Excluded from ranking; never coerced to zero!

3. Human Competition Banding

Competition is never scraped (preventing ToS violations and proxy unreliability). A human fills in top-10 observations in the table above. A band is derived only when ≥ 2 fields are recorded:

  • Low: Weak domains present AND thin results present.
  • Medium: Weak domains present OR stale incumbent pages present.
  • High / Very High: Established authoritative domains with comprehensive content.
  • Unmeasured: Fewer than 2 fields recorded. Never coerced to "easy" or zero!

📊 Understanding Your Demand Scores

🟢 Strong Evidence (≥ 0.50)

High auto-complete presence, broad alphabet expansion, and question demand. Prioritize these topics in your next articles.

🟡 Moderate Evidence (0.25 – 0.49)

Measurable interest on partial expansion axes. Excellent for secondary sections or supporting blog posts.

⚪ Weak Evidence (< 0.25)

Sparse auto-complete signal. Highly specific long-tail query or nascent emerging topic.

🔴 Insufficient / Unmeasured (⊥)

No response or rate-limited. We refuse to lie and coerce this to zero. Praman flags it as unmeasured so you can re-test.

🛡️ The Praman Integrity & Safety Guarantee

Zero SERP Scraping: We never scrape search result pages or bypass bot protections. Competition checks are 100% human-observed.

Zero Fabricated Numbers: Traditional SEO tools multiply clickstream approximations to give you "10,000 monthly volume" that often turns out to be zero in regional languages. Praman shows verified autocomplete demand mathematically.
