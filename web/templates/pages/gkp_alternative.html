Why GKP Lies About Search Volume in Regional India

Google Keyword Planner is an ad-buying inventory calculator. If local advertisers in Maharashtra or Bihar are not actively bidding on "कापूस बाजारभाव" (cotton prices) or "पीएम किसान 17 वा हप्ता", GKP's internal threshold considers that keyword commercially dormant and reports 0 – 10 monthly searches.

Yet in reality, Search Console data shows millions of impressions for these exact queries.

Praman's Pinned Demand Blend bypasses ad auction data completely. It queries Google's autocomplete matrix across 4 axes (Breadth $0.50$, Head Coverage $0.25$, Question Density $0.15$, Rank Depth $0.10$) to quantify true citizen interest.
